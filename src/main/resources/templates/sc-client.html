Test Client for SAML and OpenID Connect

This is a test client for testing SAML Identity Providers and OpenID Providers within the Sweden Connect federation. The tool is mainly used during approval processes for IdP:s/OP:s wishing to join the federation, but is also an excellent help in the development of an IdP/OP.

This instance of the tool is executing against .

To add your SAML Identity Provider to Sweden Connect, contact operations@swedenconnect.se.
